# Product scope

## Initial MVP
1. Jobs & Employment
2. Business Directory
3. Services Finder
4. Marketplace
5. Education & Scholarships
6. User profiles, location, moderation, notifications and admin controls

## Later modules
Agriculture, property and accommodation, health directory, transport and logistics, events and announcements, business promotion, community and advanced analytics.

The product is intended for Nigeria nationally. Sokoto State is the proposed first pilot, not the platform's geographic limit.
